1Introduction and data controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Commerce Visual collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of people who visit commercevisual.com.br or get in touch with us.

It is written to meet the requirements of the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the data protection law of Brazil, where Commerce Visual is established, and good privacy practices for visitors from the United States.

The data controller decides how and why personal data is processed. In this case:

Commerce Visual — Brazilian company (sole proprietorship, MEI)

CNPJ (Brazilian company number): 68.961.224/0001-01

Services: website design, online stores, custom systems and digital security

100% remote, serving clients in the US, Europe and Brazil

Privacy email: commercevisual@hotmail.com

Data protection contact: Maria, owner of Commerce Visual

When we build a website or system for a client and handle personal data of their customers, we act as a processor, following the client's instructions and the contract signed with them. This policy covers the data Commerce Visual collects as a controller.

2Data we collect

2.1 Data you send us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, email or a contact form, we may receive:

  • your name;
  • phone number and email address;
  • company name;
  • information about your project (goals, timelines, budget, references and any files you choose to send).

Please do not send special category data (such as health, religion or political opinions) or passwords through these channels. If access credentials are needed for a project, we will agree on a secure method.

2.2 Technical access data

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically logs some technical data for each visit: IP address, date and time, browser type, operating system and device. These logs are created by our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and are used for security and to keep the website running.

2.3 Preferences stored in your browser

If you choose a language or answer the cookie notice, that choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so we do not ask again. This information is not sent to us.

3How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and send quotes and proposals.
  • To deliver the services you hire: planning, building, delivering and supporting your website, online store, system or security solution.
  • To issue invoices and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and running, preventing fraud, attacks and abuse.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you.

5Cookies and local storage

What are they? Cookies and local storage are small pieces of information a website saves in your browser, for example to remember your choices.

What we use today: the Commerce Visual website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. There is no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tool installed. We only store strictly necessary preferences in your browser: your language choice and your answer to the cookie notice.

If this changes: if we ever start using analytics or marketing cookies, they will only be activated after you give consent through the cookie notice, with options to accept, reject or customize. This policy will be updated beforehand.

You can reopen the cookie notice at any time through the cookie preferences link, and you can delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

6Sharing with third parties

We never sell your personal data. We do not rent it out or pass it on to other companies for their own marketing.

We only share data with service providers we need to operate, each with its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) — website hosting and technical access logs.
  • Hostinger — domain registration and DNS.
  • Google Fonts (Google) — delivery of the fonts used on the website, which involves your browser connecting to Google's servers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you choose to talk to us on WhatsApp, the conversation goes through the WhatsApp platform.
  • Email provider (Microsoft / Outlook) — when you email us.

We may also share data when required by law, by court order or by a competent authority, or to defend our rights.

7International data transfers

Commerce Visual is based in Brazil, and some of our providers (such as GitHub, Google, Meta and Microsoft) store or process data in the United States and other countries. Because we serve clients in the US, Europe and Brazil, project data may move between these countries.

For data from the European Economic Area, transfers only take place with the safeguards required by Chapter V of the GDPR, such as:

  • adequacy decisions of the European Commission, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework when the provider is certified;
  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission;
  • transfers necessary for the performance of a contract with you, when the transfer is needed to deliver the service you hired (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR).

9How we protect data

Digital security is one of the services we offer our clients, and we apply the same care to your data.

Our main security measures:

  • HTTPS / SSL: all communication with the website is encrypted.
  • Access control: only the responsible team can access data, with strong passwords and two-factor authentication whenever the tool allows it.
  • Data minimization: we only collect and keep the data we actually use.
  • Backups: regular backups to prevent data loss.
  • Updates: systems, plugins and dependencies kept up to date to fix known vulnerabilities.
  • Good practices: client credentials (such as dashboard passwords) are exchanged through secure channels and revoked at the end of the project.

No system is 100% secure. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to you, we will notify you and the competent authorities, as required by Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR and by Brazilian law.

10Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you can at any time:

  • Access your data and receive a copy of it.
  • Rectify incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Erase your data, except where the law requires us to keep it.
  • Restrict processing in the cases provided by law.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured format to take to another provider.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, at any time.

How to exercise your rights

Email commercevisual@hotmail.com with the subject "Data subject request", saying which right you want to exercise. To protect you, we may ask for information to confirm your identity first.

Response time: we reply within 1 month (Art. 12 GDPR), which may be extended in the cases the law allows, always with notice to you. Requests are free of charge.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the EU or EEA country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR). The list of authorities is available on the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) website.

11US residents

As a good practice, we extend the following rights to visitors and clients in the United States, including those covered by state privacy laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what personal data we collect, where it comes from, why we use it and who we share it with.
  • Right to delete and right to correct your data.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of data for targeted advertising. We do not sell data and do not use marketing cookies.
  • Right to non-discrimination: you will not receive a different price or service for exercising your rights.

If we ever use marketing cookies, we will honor the browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a request not to use them.

12Children

Our services are aimed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 or direct content to them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has sent us data, contact us at the email below and we will delete it (Art. 8 GDPR).
