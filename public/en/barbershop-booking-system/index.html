Barbershop booking system

Clients book on their own and your schedule stays organized, without dozens of back-and-forth messages.

In short

  • Online booking 24/7.
  • Choice of service, barber and time.
  • Dashboard with the day's schedule.
  • Client history.

How it works for clients

  • Choose the service (haircut, beard, combo).
  • Choose their preferred barber.
  • See only available slots and confirm.
  • Get a confirmation and reschedule if needed.

How it works for the barbershop

  • Daily schedule: every barber's appointments on one screen.
  • Blocks: days off, lunch breaks and holidays.
  • Clients: visit history and preferences.
  • Reports: best-selling services and peak hours.

Your own system or a ready-made app?

Ready-made booking apps usually charge a monthly fee and show their own brand. Your own system carries your barbershop's identity and includes only the features you use. In the discovery call, we compare both options with you.
